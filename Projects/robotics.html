05 — ROBOTICS

TAMSformers Robotics

Built and programmed competition robots as part of the TAMSformers robotics team, working across mechanical design and controls.

Robotics Controls Mechanical Design Team Project

Overview

Competition robots need to be designed, built, and programmed under tight deadlines while staying reliable enough to perform consistently in matches. That meant balancing mechanical design, wiring, and software all at once as part of a team. Robotics offers a chance to be creative in multiple outlets, not just competitively.

My Contribution

Along with some friends, I helped build a go-kart out of an FRC robot frame. To ensure that children can operate it safely, we incorporated a joystick onboard and an emergency switch with a remote operator so they can stop it.
